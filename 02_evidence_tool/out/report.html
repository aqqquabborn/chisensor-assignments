8 · Definitions

Users
Counted by user_id; channel = channel of the user's first first_open (first touch). Channel attribution is not a measure of incrementality.
Qualified scan
A scan event by a user who also has first_open and qualified.
Verified first-time payer
A user with at least one transaction that has a verification event at or before the cutoff. Several verification events for one transaction are one sale.
Delivered guide
A verified transaction with a delivery event. A delivery without verified payment is not counted.
Unrefunded delivered payer
Verified, delivered, and no full refund known at the cutoff (2026-06-21 14:00:00).
Collected cash
Verified transactions × example price. Pending purchases are excluded.
Net after commission
Collected − full refunds − 15% commission on kept sales (commission reversed on refund).
Research vs events
Research participants are a separate cohort from the event-log users; feedback and events are linked by theme, never by person.
